Pipe rack models in AR

Open a model on your phone, then tap the AR icon to place it in your space. AR runs inside the browser, with nothing to install: WebXR in Chrome on Android, Quick Look in Safari on iPhone. Open the link in Chrome or Safari, not inside a QR scanner or chat app. Each page shows an AR diagnostics box at the bottom; if AR doesn’t start, the reason appears there.
